Searching the Web with 123ofnet ?

123ofnet is too large to explore link by link. It would be like trying to read the card catalogue at parliament house from A-Z. By the time you finish, the links will have changed. You can browse 123ofnet simply by clicking on the various categories listed on each page. Search 123ofnet by entering a word (or a few words) into the seach box that appears on every page in the directory. Combine the two strategies and you can "browse and then search" or "search and then browse."This search would be conducted on the all powerful open directory database.

To use the GOOGLE (tm) Search engine you will have to type the search words in the upper left hand corner search box , right below the main banner of 123ofnet.com . If it's on the web the GOOGLE (tm) search engine will find it for you

The Art of Surfing ?

The Web is more than just a reference tool. It's also a place to hang out, look around, explore. And yet sometimes it feels hard to navigate. (It's easy to get trapped into vicious web circles. Homepage to search engine to results page, and nothing new in between. But it doesn't have to be that way...)

Whether your homepage is Netscape, Microsoft, Yahoo!, Alta Vista, AOL, your ISP, your college, or your company's intranet, it pays to know where else you can go to get started. Many sites specialize in filtering the web -- weeding out the best, the newest, the weirdest sites available. Some sites feature a different link every day or week. Bookmark some of these starting points and you'll be surfing like a pro in no time.
